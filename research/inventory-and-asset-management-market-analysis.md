## 2. The four sub-categories

The phrase "inventory and asset apps" covers four different kinds of product, and buyers often mix them up:

| Category | Tracks | Typical buyer | Key capabilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Inventory / stock management** | Goods you sell or consume (SKUs, quantities) | Retail, e-commerce, wholesale, light manufacturing | Stock levels, reorder points, POs/SOs, multichannel sync, batches/serials, multi-warehouse |
| **B. Fixed / physical asset tracking** | Things you own and reuse (equipment, tools, furniture, vehicles) | Schools, hospitals, construction, offices, govt | Barcode/QR/RFID tags, check-in/out, audits, maintenance, depreciation |
| **C. IT asset management (ITAM)** | Hardware, software licences, cloud/SaaS | IT teams, MSPs | Auto-discovery, licence compliance, CMDB, ITSM/helpdesk link, MDM |
| **D. EAM / CMMS** | Heavy plant & facilities | Manufacturing, utilities, facilities | Work orders, preventive/predictive maintenance, IoT sensors |

## 5. Key trends (2025–2026)

1. **AI features are now table stakes.** Vendors ship demand forecasting, reorder suggestions, anomaly detection on stock counts, natural-language queries ("what's running low in warehouse 2?") and AI-generated item descriptions and categorisation.
2. **IoT, RFID and BLE tracking.** Asset tracking is moving from barcode scans to passive RFID and Bluetooth/GPS tags with live location. RFID is the largest technology segment.
3. **Convergence.** Inventory tools are adding asset modules, ITAM is merging with ITSM and FinOps/SaaS management, and SMB tools are growing into "lite ERP".
4. **Mobile-first.** Phone-camera barcode scanning has replaced dedicated scanners for SMBs.
5. **Usage-based pricing.** Prices are increasingly tied to orders, items or assets rather than per seat. Low entry prices can grow quickly at scale.
6. **Open source and self-hosting** (Snipe-IT, ERPNext, Odoo Community, InvenTree) attract cost-sensitive buyers and those with data-residency requirements.
7. **Compliance pressure.** E-invoicing/GST (India), software licence audits and ESG/asset lifecycle (e-waste) reporting drive purchases.

## 6. Gaps / opportunities in the market

- **The SMB middle ground.** Simple apps like Sortly lack accounting depth, and ERPs are too complex. Few products handle *both* consumable stock and reusable assets well at SMB prices.
- **Unpredictable pricing.** Buyers complain about item, order or asset caps and quote-only pricing (Asset Panda, Cin7, enterprise ITAM).
- **Local compliance + language.** Regional players (Tally, Vyapar) win in India on GST, offline use and local languages, which global SaaS tools handle less well.
- **Offline / low-connectivity use** for field sites, warehouses and rural areas.
- **Affordable RFID/BLE.** Most products that do live tracking are enterprise-priced.
- **AI that acts, not just reports.** Few products auto-create POs, schedule maintenance or reconcile audits end-to-end.

## 7. How to choose (quick guide)

| If you are… | Shortlist |
|---|---|
| Micro/small business, mostly tracking items | Sortly (free), Zoho Inventory (free) |
| Indian SMB needing GST billing + stock | Zoho Inventory, TallyPrime, Vyapar |
| E-commerce, multichannel | Ordoro, Zoho Inventory, Cin7 |
| Wholesale / light manufacturing | inFlow, Fishbowl, Odoo |
| Office/school/hospital equipment | EZOfficeInventory, Asset Panda, Reftab |
| IT team, small budget | Snipe-IT, ManageEngine, Freshservice |
| Enterprise IT | ServiceNow, Flexera, Lansweeper, Ivanti |
| Want a full ERP | Odoo, ERPNext, NetSuite, D365 Business Central |

**Evaluation checklist:** item/asset volume and pricing caps · barcode/QR/RFID support · mobile and offline use · multi-location · integrations (accounting, e-commerce, ITSM, MDM, SSO) · depreciation methods · audit trail and roles · API access · data export and lock-in · local tax compliance · total cost over 3 years.
